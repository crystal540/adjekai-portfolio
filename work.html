Selected Work

Projects, Research & Engagements

An overview of selected projects, publications, teaching and speaking engagements focused on energy development in Africa.

Projects and Programmes

SEforALL / UNOPS

Mini-grid results based financing

Co-lead implementation of an over US$30 million EU-funded results-based financing programme supporting solar mini-grid development in Sierra Leone. Work includes developer engagement, governmentcoordination, project verification and monitoring programme results.

Sustainable Energy for All

Mission 300 Market Activation

Co-lead the Market Activation workstream supporting the World Bank and and African Development Bank-led Mission 300 initiative. The work involves assessing regulatory, financial and operational barriers to distributed renewable energy investment across Sub-Saharan Africa and identifying measures to support market development.

Boston University Global DevelopmentPolicy Centre

Renewable Energy Project Preparation in Southern Africa

Researched the design of a pre-feasibility facility to supportearly-stage renewable energyand energy access projects in the southern African Development Community. The work examined how development finance institutions could help prepare projects for investment and was presented to regional DFI chief executives.

University of Cape Town, Power Futures Lab/ GET.transform

Peer Review Learning Network

Supported a peer learning programme bringing together electricity regulators and policymakers from six African contries. The programme facilitated exchanges on regulatory approaches, institutional performanceand the conditions needed to attract private investment in the power sector.

Research

Energy for Growth Hub, 2026

Four Ways African Countries Can Secure Better Outcomes from Chinese-Supported Power Projects

A memo examining how African goverments can strengthen their role in negotiating and implementing Chinese-supported power generation projects.

China Global South Project, 2026

Book for Policymakers on Chinese-Supported Power Projects in Sub-Suharan Africa.

A practical resource for policymakers examining Chinese-supported power generation projects in Sub-Suharan Africa, drawing on research into investment, negotiations and the role of African governments.

Boston University Global Development Policy Centre, 2024

Africa Takes the Wheel: The Role of Host States in Shaping Outcomes of Chinese-Supported Power Generation Projects in Africa

A working paper examining how African host governments influence the outcomes of Chinese-supported power generation projects, drawing on case studies from Ghana and Uganda.

China Global South Project, 2025

The Porcelain Jar at the End of the Rainbow

An original series authored for the China Global South Project, examining China's engagement with Africa, with a focus on energy investment and development.

Springer, 2024

Effective Regulation and the Energy Transition in Zambia

A peer-reviewed book chapter in Energy Regulattion in Africa, examining Zambia's regulatory approach to the energy transition.
