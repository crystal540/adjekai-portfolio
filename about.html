Dr. Naa Adjekai Adjei is a corporate attorney and energy specialist working at the intersection of infrastructure finance, regulation, and law.

She holds a PhD in Business Administration from the University of Cape Town, with research on the enabling conditions for Chinese investment in power generation infrastructure across Sub-Saharan Africa. At Sustainable Energy for All (SEforALL), she co-led delivery of the World Bank and AfDB-led Mission 300 and a $30 million EU-funded results-based financing programme for solar mini-grids in Sierra Leone.

Her legal experience practicing as an attourney at Cliffe Dekker Hofmeyr (formely in alliance with DLA Piper) and Webber Wentzel (formely in alliance with Linklaters) allows her to critically analyse the agreements underpinning energy projects, ensuring project agreements are structured to protect the interets of investors, governments and end-users alike.

Her research has been published through Springer, Boston University, the China Global south Project, where she served as the inaugural Non-Resident Fellow for Africa, and the Energy for Growth Hub, where she is also a Fellow.

She is available for consulting, advisory, teaching and speaking engagements on Chinese energy investment, regulatory design and infrastructure delivery in emerging markets.
