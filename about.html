Dr. Naa Adjekai Adjei is a corporate attorney and energy specialist working at the intersection of infrastructure finance, regulation, and policy.

She holds a PhD in Business Administration from the University of Cape Town, with research on the enabling conditions for Chinese investment in power generation infrastructure across Sub-Saharan Africa. At Sustainable Energy for All (SEforALL), she co-leads delivery of the World Bank and AfDB-led Mission 300 and a $20 million EU-funded results-based financing programme for solar mini-grids in Sierra Leone.

Her legal practising at Cliffe Dekker Hofmeyr and Webber Wentzel (in alliance with Linklaters) allows her to critically analyse the agreements underpinning energy projects — ensuring power purchase agreements and loan covenants are structured to protect the interests of investors, governments, and end-users alike. Her research has been published through Springer, Boston University, and the China Global South Project, where she served as inaugural Non-Resident Fellow for Africa.

She is available for consulting, advisory, and speaking engagements on Chinese energy investment, regulatory design, and infrastructure delivery in Sub-Saharan Africa.
